"induction: commutativity of times needs permutative lemmas of plus, which cannot be used as rewrite rules"
sum y # plus(_0, y) -> y;
sum x, y # plus(suc(x), y) -> suc(plus(x, y));
sum y # times(_0, y) -> _0;
sum x, y # times(suc(x), y) -> plus(y, times(x, y));

induct x, y # times(x, y) = times(y, x)
