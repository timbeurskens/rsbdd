"
A non-commutative monoid

The situation

    An operation m is associative: m(m(x, y), z) = m(x, m(y, z)).
    It has an identity element e: m(e, x) = x and m(x, e) = x.

The question is: is m commutative, so that m(x, y) = m(y, x)?

---

No: the countermodel is the table of m over three elements. One of them is the identity, and the other two are zeros
on one side: m(x, a) = a and m(x, b) = b for every x, so m(a, b) = b but m(b, a) = a. Every monoid with at most two
elements is commutative, so the search fails for one and two elements first.

Run with:
    rsbdd examples/fol/monoid.txt --prove
"

(
    (forall x, y, z # m(m(x, y), z) = m(x, m(y, z))) &
    (forall x # m(e, x) = x & m(x, e) = x)
) => forall x, y # m(x, y) = m(y, x)
