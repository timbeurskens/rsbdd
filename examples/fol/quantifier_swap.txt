Everybody loves somebody

The situation

    Everybody loves somebody.

The question is: is there somebody whom everybody loves?

---

No: swapping 'for all' and 'there exists' changes the meaning. The countermodel has two people who each love only
themselves. The converse, 'if somebody is loved by everybody, then everybody loves somebody', is a theorem.

Run with:
    rsbdd examples/fol/quantifier_swap.txt --prove
"

(forall x # exists y # loves(x, y)) => exists y # forall x # loves(x, y)
