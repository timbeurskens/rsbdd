"
Schubert's Steamroller

The situation

    Wolves, foxes, birds, caterpillars, and snails are animals, and there are some of each of them.
    Also there are some grains, and grains are plants.
    Every animal either likes to eat all plants or all animals much smaller than itself that like to eat some plants.
    Caterpillars and snails are much smaller than birds, which are much smaller than foxes,
    which in turn are much smaller than wolves.
    Wolves do not like to eat foxes or grains, while birds like to eat caterpillars but not snails.
    Caterpillars and snails like to eat some plants.

The question is: is there an animal that likes to eat a grain-eating animal?

From: Pelletier, Seventy-five problems for testing automatic theorem provers (1986), problem 47
      Schubert's challenge to automated theorem provers (1978), TPTP problem PUZ031

---

The facts imply the conclusion, but no animal is named: the prover has to find the eater (the wolf, eating the fox
that eats grain, or the fox, eating the bird that eats grain) from the Skolem constants of the 'there are some' facts.

Run with:
    rsbdd examples/fol/steamroller.txt --prove
"

(
    (forall x # wolf(x) => animal(x)) & (exists x # wolf(x)) &
    (forall x # fox(x) => animal(x)) & (exists x # fox(x)) &
    (forall x # bird(x) => animal(x)) & (exists x # bird(x)) &
    (forall x # caterpillar(x) => animal(x)) & (exists x # caterpillar(x)) &
    (forall x # snail(x) => animal(x)) & (exists x # snail(x)) &
    (forall x # grain(x) => plant(x)) & (exists x # grain(x)) &

    (forall x # animal(x) =>
        (forall y # plant(y) => eats(x, y)) |
        (forall y # animal(y) & smaller(y, x) & (exists z # plant(z) & eats(y, z)) => eats(x, y))) &

    (forall x, y # bird(y) & (caterpillar(x) | snail(x)) => smaller(x, y)) &
    (forall x, y # fox(y) & bird(x) => smaller(x, y)) &
    (forall x, y # wolf(y) & fox(x) => smaller(x, y)) &

    (forall x, y # wolf(x) & (fox(y) | grain(y)) => -eats(x, y)) &
    (forall x, y # bird(x) & caterpillar(y) => eats(x, y)) &
    (forall x, y # bird(x) & snail(y) => -eats(x, y)) &
    (forall x # caterpillar(x) | snail(x) => exists y # plant(y) & eats(x, y))
) => exists x, y # animal(x) & animal(y) & eats(x, y) & forall z # grain(z) => eats(y, z)
