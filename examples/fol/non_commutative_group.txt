"
A non-commutative group

The situation

    An operation m is associative: m(m(x, y), z) = m(x, m(y, z)).
    It has a right identity e: m(x, e) = x.
    Every element x has a right inverse i(x): m(x, i(x)) = e.

The question is: is m commutative, so that m(x, y) = m(y, x)?

---

No: the countermodel is the smallest non-commutative group, the permutations of three objects, with six elements.
The Skolem constants are two elements that do not commute. Every group with at most five elements is commutative, so
the search fails for one to five elements first. This is the Mace4 example that finds a non-commutative group.

Run with:
    rsbdd examples/fol/non_commutative_group.txt --prove
"

(
    (forall x, y, z # m(m(x, y), z) = m(x, m(y, z))) &
    (forall x # m(x, e) = x) &
    (forall x # m(x, i(x)) = e)
) => forall x, y # m(x, y) = m(y, x)
