"
Caramel vanilla coffee helps people stay awake

The situation

    Coffee is a beverage. Vanilla syrup and caramel syrup are syrups.
    The mixture of a syrup and a beverage is a beverage, and the mixture of a syrup and a syrup is a syrup.
    The mixture of coffee and any syrup helps people stay awake.

The question is: does caramel vanilla coffee help people stay awake?

From: TPTP problem PUZ139_1 (Arhami, 2014)

---

Yes. mixture is polymorphic: its first argument is the type of the mixture, so mixture(syrup, caramel_syrup,
vanilla_syrup) is a syrup, and the coffee mixed with it helps people stay awake.

No variable is an operand of =, so the types are monotonic and the prover erases them: the proof instantiates the
axiom with the syrup directly.

Run with:
    rsbdd examples/fol/coffee.txt --prove
"

let beverage: type;
let syrup: type;
let coffee: beverage;
let vanilla_syrup, caramel_syrup: syrup;
let mixture: pi T # (T, syrup) -> T;
let help_people_stay_awake: (beverage) -> bool;

(forall s: syrup # help_people_stay_awake(mixture(beverage, coffee, s))) =>
    help_people_stay_awake(mixture(beverage, coffee, mixture(syrup, caramel_syrup, vanilla_syrup)))
