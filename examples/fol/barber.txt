"
The barber paradox

The situation

    A village barber shaves exactly those villagers who do not shave themselves.

The question is: can such a barber exist?

From: Bertrand Russell (1918), as an illustration of Russell's paradox
      Pelletier, Seventy-five problems for testing automatic theorem provers (1986), problem 39

---

No such barber exists: should the barber shave himself? If he does, he must not, and if he does not, he must.
The proof instantiates 'everyone the barber shaves' with the barber himself.

Run with:
    rsbdd examples/fol/barber.txt --prove
"

-(exists x # barber(x) & forall y # (shaves(x, y) <=> -shaves(y, y)))
