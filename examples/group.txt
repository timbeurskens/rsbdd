"
Theorem proving in group theory by term rewriting.

The group axioms below (with unit one, product mul and inverse inv) are not a convergent rewrite system: they cannot
rewrite mul(x, one), for example. Knuth-Bendix completion (Knuth & Bendix, 1970) adds the missing rules, such as

    mul(x, one) -> x,   inv(inv(x)) -> x,   inv(mul(x, y)) -> mul(inv(y), inv(x))

Two terms are equal in every group if and only if their normal forms in the completed system are identical, so the
theorems below are proven by rewriting both sides of each equation to normal form.

The last theorem combines rewriting with the solver: the unknown p(..) is identified modulo the group axioms.
The formula is valid, resulting in a single 'true' node in the BDD.

Run with:
    rsbdd examples/group.txt
"

sum x # mul(one, x) -> x;
sum x # mul(inv(x), x) -> one;
sum x, y, z # mul(mul(x, y), z) -> mul(x, mul(y, z));

"the inverse of a product is the product of the inverses in reverse order"
inv(mul(a, b)) = mul(inv(b), inv(a));

"cancellation"
mul(mul(a, inv(b)), b) = a;

"the inverse of a conjugate is the conjugate of the inverse"
inv(mul(mul(g, a), inv(g))) = mul(g, mul(inv(a), inv(g)));

"properties of an unknown predicate p hold modulo the group axioms"
p(mul(a, inv(a))) => p(inv(inv(one)))
