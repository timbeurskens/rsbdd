"
Theorem proving in group theory by term rewriting.

The group axioms (with unit one, product mul and inverse inv)

    mul(one, x) = x,   mul(inv(x), x) = one,   mul(mul(x, y), z) = mul(x, mul(y, z))

are turned into the convergent rewrite system below by Knuth-Bendix completion (Knuth & Bendix, 1970).
Two terms are equal in every group if and only if their normal forms are identical, so the theorems below
are proven by rewriting both sides of each equation to normal form.

The last theorem combines rewriting with the solver: the unknown p(..) is identified modulo the group axioms.
The formula is valid, resulting in a single 'true' node in the BDD.

Run with:
    rsbdd examples/group.txt
"

sum x # mul(one, x) -> x;
sum x # mul(x, one) -> x;
sum x # mul(inv(x), x) -> one;
sum x # mul(x, inv(x)) -> one;
sum x, y, z # mul(mul(x, y), z) -> mul(x, mul(y, z));
sum x, y # mul(inv(x), mul(x, y)) -> y;
sum x, y # mul(x, mul(inv(x), y)) -> y;
inv(one) -> one;
sum x # inv(inv(x)) -> x;
sum x, y # inv(mul(x, y)) -> mul(inv(y), inv(x));

"the inverse of a product is the product of the inverses in reverse order"
inv(mul(a, b)) = mul(inv(b), inv(a));

"cancellation"
mul(mul(a, inv(b)), b) = a;

"the inverse of a conjugate is the conjugate of the inverse"
inv(mul(mul(g, a), inv(g))) = mul(g, mul(inv(a), inv(g)));

"properties of an unknown predicate p hold modulo the group axioms"
p(mul(a, inv(a))) => p(inv(inv(one)))
