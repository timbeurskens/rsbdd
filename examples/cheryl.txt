"
Cheryl's Birthday

The situation

    Albert and Bernard just became friends with Cheryl, and they want to know when her birthday is.
    Cheryl gives them a list of 10 possible dates:

        May 15,    May 16,    May 19
        June 17,   June 18
        July 14,   July 16
        August 14, August 15, August 17

    Cheryl then tells Albert only the month, and Bernard only the day of her birthday.

    1) Albert: I don't know when Cheryl's birthday is, but I know that Bernard does not know too.
    2) Bernard: At first I didn't know when Cheryl's birthday is, but I know now.
    3) Albert: Then I also know when Cheryl's birthday is.

The question is: when is Cheryl's birthday?

From: Singapore and Asian Schools Math Olympiad (SASMO) 2015
      https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheryl%27s_Birthday

---

The month and day of a date w are functions. Like val(v, n) in peano_solve.txt, the unknowns month(w, m) and
day(w, d) state that the month of w is m and the day of w is d. They have no rules, so the solver chooses their
interpretation. Cheryl's birthday is the date Cheryl.

Someone knows the birthday w if every candidate date they cannot tell apart from w is w itself. The other candidate
is the date other(w), and 'every candidate' is a quantifier over its unknowns: forall month(other(w), _), day(other(w), _).
Every statement keeps the candidate dates for which it is true.

Run with:
    rsbdd examples/cheryl.txt -t -ft
"

date(May, D15) -> true;
date(May, D16) -> true;
date(May, D19) -> true;
date(June, D17) -> true;
date(June, D18) -> true;
date(July, D14) -> true;
date(July, D16) -> true;
date(August, D14) -> true;
date(August, D15) -> true;
date(August, D17) -> true;

"A date w has exactly one month and day, which are on Cheryl's list"
sum w # candidate(List, w) ->
    exists m, d # date(m, d) & month(w, m) & day(w, d) &
        forall m2, d2 # date(m2, d2) => (month(w, m2) => m2 = m) & (day(w, d2) => d2 = d);

"Albert is told the month, Bernard is told the day"
sum w, v # same_month(w, v) -> exists m, d # date(m, d) & month(w, m) & month(v, m);
sum w, v # same_day(w, v) -> exists m, d # date(m, d) & day(w, d) & day(v, d);
sum w, v # indistinguishable(Albert, w, v) -> same_month(w, v);
sum w, v # indistinguishable(Bernard, w, v) -> same_day(w, v);

"Person p knows the date w among the candidates s if every candidate p cannot tell apart from w is w"
sum p, s, w # knows(p, s, w) ->
    forall month(other(w), _), day(other(w), _) #
        candidate(s, other(w)) & indistinguishable(p, w, other(w)) => same_month(w, other(w)) & same_day(w, other(w));

"1) Albert: I don't know when Cheryl's birthday is, but I know that Bernard does not know too."
sum w # candidate(S1, w) ->
    candidate(List, w) & -knows(Albert, List, w) &
    forall month(other(w), _), day(other(w), _) #
        candidate(List, other(w)) & indistinguishable(Albert, w, other(w)) => -knows(Bernard, List, other(w));

"2) Bernard: At first I didn't know when Cheryl's birthday is, but I know now."
sum w # candidate(S2, w) -> candidate(S1, w) & -knows(Bernard, List, w) & knows(Bernard, S1, w);

"3) Albert: Then I also know when Cheryl's birthday is."
sum w # candidate(S3, w) -> candidate(S2, w) & knows(Albert, S2, w);

candidate(S3, Cheryl)
