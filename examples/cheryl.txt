"
Cheryl's Birthday

The situation

    Albert and Bernard just became friends with Cheryl, and they want to know when her birthday is.
    Cheryl gives them a list of 10 possible dates:

        May 15,    May 16,    May 19
        June 17,   June 18
        July 14,   July 16
        August 14, August 15, August 17

    Cheryl then tells Albert only the month, and Bernard only the day of her birthday.

    1) Albert: I don't know when Cheryl's birthday is, but I know that Bernard does not know too.
    2) Bernard: At first I didn't know when Cheryl's birthday is, but I know now.
    3) Albert: Then I also know when Cheryl's birthday is.

The question is: when is Cheryl's birthday?

From: Singapore and Asian Schools Math Olympiad (SASMO) 2015
      https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheryl%27s_Birthday

---

Every statement narrows down the set of candidate dates, defined by rewrite rules:
- s1: Albert's month has no date with a day that is unique, so Bernard cannot know (rules out May and June).
- s2: of the dates left after s1, Bernard's day occurs in only one month (rules out the 14th).
- s3: of the dates left after s2, Albert's month has only one date (rules out August).

The unknowns birthday(m, d) are true exactly for the dates that remain after all three statements.

Run with:
    rsbdd examples/cheryl.txt -t -ft
"

date(May, D15) -> true;
date(May, D16) -> true;
date(May, D19) -> true;
date(June, D17) -> true;
date(June, D18) -> true;
date(July, D14) -> true;
date(July, D16) -> true;
date(August, D14) -> true;
date(August, D15) -> true;
date(August, D17) -> true;

"1) Every day in Albert's month also occurs in another month, so Bernard does not know"
sum m # bernard_unsure(m) -> forall d # date(m, d) => exists m2 # -(m2 = m) & date(m2, d);
sum m, d # s1(m, d) -> date(m, d) & bernard_unsure(m);

"2) Bernard now knows: his day occurs in only one month of the remaining dates"
sum m, d # s2(m, d) -> s1(m, d) & forall m2 # s1(m2, d) => m2 = m;

"3) Albert now knows: his month has only one of the remaining dates"
sum m, d # s3(m, d) -> s2(m, d) & forall d2 # s2(m, d2) => d2 = d;

forall m, d # date(m, d) => (birthday(m, d) <=> s3(m, d))
