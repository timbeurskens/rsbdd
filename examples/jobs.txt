"
The Jobs Puzzle

The situation

    There are four people: Roberta, Thelma, Steve, and Pete.
    Among them they hold eight different jobs, and each holds exactly two jobs.
    The jobs are: chef, guard, nurse, telephone operator, police officer (either gender), teacher, actor, and boxer.
    The job of nurse is held by a male.
    The husband of the chef is the telephone operator.
    Roberta is not a boxer.
    Pete has no education past the ninth grade.
    Roberta, the chef, and the police officer went golfing together.

The question is: who holds which job?

From: Wos, Overbeek, Lusk & Boyle, Automated Reasoning: Introduction and Applications (1984)
      TPTP problem PUZ019

---

holds(p, j) is the unknown: every person holds exactly two jobs, and every job is held by exactly one person.

Most clues are implicit, as in the original puzzle:
- Roberta and Thelma are female, Steve and Pete are male.
- An actor is male, and the nurse is male.
- The chef has a husband, so the chef is female, and the telephone operator is male.
- A nurse, a police officer and a teacher need more than a ninth-grade education, so Pete is none of them.
- Roberta, the chef and the police officer are three different people.

The chef is female but not Roberta, so Thelma is the chef. The police officer is neither Roberta, Thelma nor Pete,
so it is Steve. Pete, the remaining male, cannot be the nurse, so Steve is the nurse as well, and Pete is the
operator and the actor. Roberta is not the boxer, so Thelma is, and Roberta is the guard and the teacher.
The truth table has a single row.

Run with:
    rsbdd examples/jobs.txt -t -ft
"

person(Roberta) -> true;
person(Thelma) -> true;
person(Steve) -> true;
person(Pete) -> true;

male(Steve) -> true;
male(Pete) -> true;

job(Chef) -> true;
job(Guard) -> true;
job(Nurse) -> true;
job(Operator) -> true;
job(Police) -> true;
job(Teacher) -> true;
job(Actor) -> true;
job(Boxer) -> true;

"Every person holds two jobs, and every job is held by one person"
forall p # person(p) => [holds(p, Chef), holds(p, Guard), holds(p, Nurse), holds(p, Operator),
                         holds(p, Police), holds(p, Teacher), holds(p, Actor), holds(p, Boxer)] = 2;
forall j # job(j) => [holds(Roberta, j), holds(Thelma, j), holds(Steve, j), holds(Pete, j)] = 1;

"The nurse and the actor are male"
forall p # person(p) => (holds(p, Nurse) | holds(p, Actor)) => male(p);

"The husband of the chef is the telephone operator"
forall p # person(p) => (holds(p, Chef) => -male(p)) & (holds(p, Operator) => male(p));

"Roberta is not a boxer"
-holds(Roberta, Boxer);

"Pete has no education past the ninth grade"
-holds(Pete, Nurse) & -holds(Pete, Police) & -holds(Pete, Teacher);

"Roberta, the chef and the police officer went golfing together"
-holds(Roberta, Chef) & -holds(Roberta, Police);
forall p # person(p) => -(holds(p, Chef) & holds(p, Police))
